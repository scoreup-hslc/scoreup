FAQ

Is this as per the current HSLC syllabus?

Yes. It follows the current ASSEB (SEBA) Class 10 General Mathematics syllabus. Construction is not included because it has been removed. Old-syllabus questions are marked and kept for practice.

Which years are covered?

HSLC papers from 2013 to 2026. The 2022 paper is not included because it is not available.

What is the difference between the three books?

The complete e-book has short notes plus all solved PYQs. "PYQ with solutions" has only the solved questions, no notes. "PYQ questions only" has the questions without solutions, for practice and self-tests.
